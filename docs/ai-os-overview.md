# AI-OS Overview

## Summary
AI-OS is a customizable, cloud-backed business workspace that gives practitioners, freelancers, solo founders, and local businesses a complete operational command center — pre-configured for their specific domain, deployed under their own brand, and enhanced with AI that understands their work.

## Definition
AI-OS is a domain-aware business operating system that adapts to a client's specific profession, providing tailored workflows, terminology, and AI assistance rather than requiring clients to adapt to generic software tools.

## Key Ideas
- Domain-specific customization replaces one-size-fits-all SaaS tools
- AI assistant trained on client's specific business context and vocabulary
- Done-for-you setup eliminates client configuration burden
- Isolated data storage per client ensures privacy and portability
- Human-guided onboarding reduces churn compared to self-serve models
- One-time setup fee with low monthly hosting creates sustainable revenue

## Evidence and Findings
From the AI-OS Master Spec v2.0:
- AI-OS is positioned as "a complete business workspace, built specifically for [your profession], deployed under your name, running in the cloud — set up by a specialist in one week."
- The system targets specific verticals: financial advisors, freelance consultants, local service businesses, and solo e-commerce founders
- Unlike generic tools where "AI doesn't know your business," AI-OS provides "AI trained on domain-specific context"
- Clients don't adapt to the tool; instead "the tool adapts to the client's workflow"

## Areas of Agreement
- All sources agree AI-OS provides domain-specific customization
- Consensus that AI-OS uses a done-for-you setup model
- Agreement that each client gets isolated infrastructure (Supabase project)
- Shared understanding that AI-OS includes AI assistance tailored to the client's profession

## Areas of Disagreement
| Topic | Source Positions | Possible Explanation |
|-------|------------------|----------------------|
| Pricing ranges | Varies from $800-$2,500 setup fees | Different vertical complexities justify different pricing |
| AI provider flexibility | Some docs mention OpenAI/Anthropic/Groq options | Evolution of the spec over time shows increasing provider flexibility |
