# AI-OS Pricing & Revenue Model

## Summary
AI-OS uses a tiered pricing model with service packages (Starter, Professional, Complete) and monthly retainer options (Basic, Standard, Priority) to create predictable recurring revenue while accommodating different client needs and budgets.

## Definition
The AI-OS Pricing & Revenue Model combines one-time setup fees with recurring monthly retainers, feature add-ons, and future licensing options to create a sustainable business model that rewards long-term client relationships while providing entry points for different customer segments.

## Key Ideas
### Service Packages (One-Time Setup)
1. **Starter — $800**
   - 1 vertical deployment
   - Up to 4 core modules
   - Basic branding (logo + colors)
   - Default AI persona
   - 1-hour handover call
   - 1 week of setup time

2. **Professional — $1,500**
   - 1 vertical deployment
   - Up to 8 modules (including invoices, time tracking, document vault)
   - Full branding customization
   - Custom AI persona with domain-specific context
   - Data import (up to 500 contacts/records)
   - Branded PDF templates
   - 2-week setup, recorded walkthrough video

3. **Complete — $2,500**
   - Everything in Professional
   - Custom module configuration (tailored field names, custom workflows)
   - Full data migration from existing tools
   - 2 × 1-hour training sessions
   - Priority support for first 3 months

### Monthly Retainer Tiers
| Tier | Price | Includes |
|------|-------|----------|
| Basic | $50/month | Supabase hosting pass-through + uptime monitoring |
| Standard | $99/month | Above + minor config updates (1–2/month) + email support |
| Priority | $149/month | Above + 1 module update/month + priority response (4hr) |

### Future Revenue Streams
- **Feature Add-ons** (Later): Extra modules or custom AI workflows ($200–$600 per add-on)
- **Self-Hosted License** (Future — Year 2):
  - Developer License: $349 one-time — full source, no support
  - Agency License: $799 one-time — right to deploy for clients, no white-label restriction

### Unit Economics (Conservative Projections)
```
Month 1–3 (building):  1 client/month × $1,200 setup = $1,200
Month 4–6 (ramping):   2 clients/month + 4 retainers = ~$2,900/month
Month 7–12 (steady):   2 setups + 12 retainers = ~$3,600–$5,400/month
Year 2+:               3 setups + 20 retainers = ~$6,000–$8,000/month
```

From AI-OS-Master-Spec-v2.md Sections 2 (Business Model) and 15 (Pricing & Revenue Model):
- Detailed breakdown of service packages and what each includes

- Unit economics table showing conservative growth projections

## Areas of Agreement
- All sources confirm the tiered service package approach
- Consensus on monthly retainer creating predictable recurring revenue
- Agreement that setup fees vary based on vertical complexity and included modules
- Shared understanding that the model balances upfront revenue with long-term retention

## Areas of Disagreement
| Topic | Source Positions | Possible Explanation |
|-------|------------------|----------------------|
| Specific price points | Some sections mention slightly different numbers | Evolution of pricing strategy during development |
| Add-on pricing range | Variations in what's considered standard add-on cost | Different complexity levels for various modules |
| Self-hosted license timing | Some suggest immediate availability, others Year 2 | Evolution of thinking as product stabilizes |
