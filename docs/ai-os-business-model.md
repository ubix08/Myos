# AI-OS Business Model

## Summary
AI-OS uses a done-for-you setup model with multiple revenue streams: upfront setup fees, monthly hosting/retainer fees, feature add-ons, and future self-hosted licensing.

## Definition
The AI-OS business model centers on Rachid acting as a specialist deployer who configures and deploys working systems for clients, rather than just providing software access. Clients pay for expertise in configuring and deploying a working system tailored to their specific domain.

## Key Ideas
- **Core Model: Done-for-You Setup** - Client engages → Discovery call → You configure vertical workspace → Deploy on their domain → Handover + training → Monthly retainer
- **Four Revenue Streams**:
  1. Setup Fee (Primary) - $800-$2,500 for full deployment
  2. Monthly Hosting + Support Retainer - $50-$150/month
  3. Feature Add-ons (Later) - $200-$600 per add-on
  4. Self-Hosted License (Later) - $299-$499 one-time for developers/agencies
- **Unit Economics** - Conservative projections show growth from $1,200/month initially to $6,000-$8,000/month by Year 2+
- **Pricing Philosophy** - Setup fee based on domain complexity; retainer builds predictable recurring revenue

## Evidence and Findings
From AI-OS-Master-Spec-v2.md:
- Setup fee scope includes: Supabase project, domain, vertical config, branding, AI setup, data import, onboarding training
- Monthly retainer scope: Supabase hosting costs pass-through + margin + minor updates + support
- Feature add-ons example: Extra modules or custom AI workflows built for specific client processes
- Self-hosted license: Sell codebase as one-time license with documentation only (no support)
- Unit economics table shows Month 1-3: 1 client/month × $1,200 setup = $1,200; Month 7-12: 2 setups + 12 retainers = ~$3,600-$5,400/month

## Areas of Agreement
- All sources confirm the done-for-you primary revenue model
- Consensus on multiple revenue streams (setup, retainer, add-ons, licensing)
- Agreement that monthly retainer creates predictable recurring revenue base
- Shared understanding that setup fee varies by domain complexity ($800-$2,500 range)

## Areas of Disagreement
| Topic | Source Positions | Possible Explanation |
|-------|------------------|----------------------|
| Self-hosted license timing | Some suggest Year 2, others less specific | Evolution of thinking as product matures |
| Add-on pricing | $200-$600 range mentioned | Different complexity levels justify different pricing |
